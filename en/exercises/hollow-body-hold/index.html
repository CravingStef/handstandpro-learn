Hollow body hold

The handstand shape, lying on your back. Lower back pressed into the floor, shoulders and legs lifted a few centimeters, your body forms a shallow shell, hollow on the belly side.

If your lower back lifts off the floor here, it will arch upside down too.

How to do it

  1. Lie on your back, arms by your sides, and press your lower back into the floor.
  2. Lift your shoulders and legs a few centimeters, legs straight.
  3. Reach your arms overhead once the position holds without your back working.
  4. Keep your lower back glued down the whole time, and breathe.

The mistake to watch

The lower back lifting off. That's the whole drill. As soon as a gap opens under your lower back, you're no longer doing a hollow, you're holding an arch. Bend one knee, or bring your arms back by your sides, and start again from there. A short hold with the back flat beats a long one in an arch.

A coach in your ear

Upside down, you can't read a screen. In the app, a voice guides you through the exercise and tells you what to fix right when you need it. For example, as soon as your legs drift toward the floor:

“Keep your lower back glued down”
