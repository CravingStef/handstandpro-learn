Arm circles

The most ordinary drill in the app, and the one you'll do most often: it opens almost every session, from the first level to the last. Sixteen circles, under a minute.

In a handstand, your shoulders carry your weight with your arms straight overhead, at the end of their range. The circles take them through that position several times, with no load, before you put your whole weight on it.

How to do it

  1. Standing, reach both arms straight out to the sides at shoulder height.
  2. Make 8 large circles backward.
  3. Then make 8 large circles forward.

Start small if your shoulders are stiff, and make the circles bigger as you go. On the last ones, your arms should brush your ears at the top and your thighs at the bottom.

Two mistakes that make it useless

Spinning fast in small circles. That warms up your arms, not your shoulders. What counts is the pass over your head, and it only happens if the circle is big.

Bending your elbows. A bent elbow shortens the lever and lets the shoulder off the hook. Keep your arms long, as if someone were pulling on your fingers.

A coach in your ear

Upside down, you can't read a screen. In the app, a voice guides you through the exercise and tells you what to fix right when you need it. For example, when your elbows start to bend:

“Keep your arms long and straight”
