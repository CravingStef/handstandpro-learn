Handstand exercises

The exercises from the Handstand Pro app, each with a video of the movement, the steps to do it and the mistakes that spoil it. The catalog has 18 of the app's 94.

The hardest part is knowing what to do each day. With the app, you start the session and it handles the rest: it picks the exercises, counts the seconds and the sets for you, and moves you on as soon as you pass the test.
